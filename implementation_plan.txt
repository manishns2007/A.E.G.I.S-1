# Implementation Plan: Project A.E.G.I.S. (Agentic Environmental Graphing & Intelligence System)

Project **A.E.G.I.S.** is a pitch-ready, real-time working prototype built for the **Hac'KP 2026 Kerala Police Cyberdome Hackathon** under the **Agentic Child Protection Investigation Assistant (ACPIA)** track. 

The core mission of A.E.G.I.S. is to revolutionize digital forensics: **shifting away from traumatizing victim pixel-matching toward analyzing invisible environmental physics and spatial geometry** to automatically detect AI-generated/synthetic media and cross-correlate real-world criminal threats while maintaining full compliance with **Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** for legal admissibility.

---

## Technical Architecture & Core Modules

1. **Automated Agentic Privacy Shield (`privacy_shield.py`)**
   - Implements immediate face and body blurring using MediaPipe Face Detection & Pose / OpenCV Haar Cascade fallback upon file ingestion.
   - Preserves 100% of background environmental details while censoring human subjects to safeguard investigator mental health.

2. **Electrical Network Frequency (ENF) Physics Analyzer (`enf_analyzer.py`)**
   - Processes video frame-by-frame, extracting mean spatial luminance time-series signals.
   - Computes Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and Power Spectral Density (PSD) via `scipy.fft` & `scipy.signal` to isolate the **50 Hz grid frequency hum** (Indian power grid standard).
   - If 50 Hz peak / harmonic ratio is absent or anomalous, flags video as **Synthetic AI Diffusion Fabrication**. If present, authenticates real-world recording.

3. **Corneal Specular Topology Analyzer (`corneal_analyzer.py`)**
   - Detects eye regions using MediaPipe Face Mesh landmarks in high-res static images and multi-frame vertical layouts.
   - Isolates left and right corneal specular light reflections (glints) using adaptive thresholding and contour moments.
   - Calculates 2D shape descriptors, surface area ratio, and reflection symmetry confidence index to detect asymmetric, physically impossible diffusion-model light artifacts.

4. **Visuo-Acoustic Knowledge Graphing (`vlm_extractor.py` & `knowledge_graph.py`)**
   - Uses Google Gemini 2.5/1.5 Flash Vision API (with structured fallback parser) to scan the **redacted background environment** (bedsheets, wall patterns, electrical outlets, ceiling fans, furniture, architectural fixtures).
   - Outputs JSON entities & spatial attributes.
   - Constructs multi-case knowledge graph using **NetworkX** and **PyVis/Plotly** to correlate disparate case files visually.

5. **BSA 2023 Section 63 Legal Docket Generator (`legal_docket.py`)**
   - Generates SHA-256 cryptographic chain-of-custody hashes for ingested files and processing logs.
   - Formats a plain-English, court-admissible forensic docket under **Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023**.

6. **Interactive Dashboard (`app.py` & `components/`)**
   - Built with **Streamlit** featuring a sleek Kerala Police Cyberdome Tactical Dark Theme (`style.css`).
   - Includes full live interactive tabs, sample test assets (authentic vs synthetic video & image samples generated algorithmically on-the-fly), and real-time execution feedback.
